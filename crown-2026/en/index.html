Before (Rules Book)

7.3.1 The penalties for lack of combativeness will follow the sequence below after the referee considers one or both athletes been under the situations described in item 6.2.1. • The referee shall count 20 (twenty) consecutive seconds and, in accordance of 1.4.1, signal lack of combativeness with the verbal command “Lute,” referring to the penalty to be applied to the athlete, in accordance with the sequence described in item 7.2.

At The Crown 2026

At The Crown 2026, the penalties for lack of combativeness (stalling) will follow the sequence below after the referee considers one or both athletes been under the situations described in item 6.2.1. • The referee shall count 10 (ten) consecutive seconds and, in accordance of 1.4.1, signal lack of combativeness with the verbal command “Lute,” referring to the penalty to be applied to the athlete, in accordance with the sequence described in item 7.2.

In short

at The Crown 2026, the countdown for the lack of combativeness (stalling) penalty is 10 (ten) consecutive seconds, instead of 20 (twenty) seconds.

Applies to

all athletes.

2

Double guard pull (6.2.1 C)

all athletes.

Before (Rules Book)

6.2.1 C) When both athletes pull guard at the same time, the referee will start a 20 second countdown. If at end of this 20 second countdown, even if the athletes are moving, one of the athletes does not reach the top position, does not have a submission in hold,or is not imminently completing a point scoring move, the referee will stop the fight and give a penalty to both athletes. In this situation, the referee will restart the combat in standing position.

At The Crown 2026

6.2.1 C) When both athletes pull guard at the same time (double guard pull), the referee will start a 20 second countdown. If at the end of this 20 second countdown neither athlete has reached the top position or scored, the referee will stop the fight, give a penalty to both athletes and restart the combat in standing position. The fight will be stopped regardless of whether a submission is in hold or a point scoring move is imminent.

In short

if at the end of the 20 seconds neither athlete has reached the top position or scored, the referee stops the match, penalizes both athletes and restarts standing – even if a submission is in hold or a point-scoring move is imminent.

Applies to

all athletes.

3

Lapel or belt grip with leg wrapped (6.2.1 G)

all athletes.

Before (Rules Book)

New rule (no previous version in the current Rules Book).

At The Crown 2026

6.2.1 G) If an athlete grips the lapel or the belt of their opponent and wraps their own leg in a controlling position, the athlete will have 20 (twenty) seconds to pursue positional progression before receiving a penalty for lack of combativeness, as described in rule 6.2.1. An athlete will receive a penalty, as described in rule 7.3.1, if positional progression has not been achieved after 20 (twenty) seconds, regardless of intention.

In short

if an athlete grips the opponent’s lapel or belt and wraps their own leg in a controlling position, they have 20 seconds to progress; if not, they receive a lack of combativeness penalty (7.3.1), regardless of intention.

Applies to

all athletes.
